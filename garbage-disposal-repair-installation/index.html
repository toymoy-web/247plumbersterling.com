Common Problems

Signs Your Disposal Needs Attention

A disposal problem is usually one of a few common issues, and telling them apart matters for whether a quick fix or a full swap makes more sense. Given how affordable a new unit typically is, we'll always give you an honest comparison rather than defaulting to the more expensive repair path.

Humming But Not Spinning

Usually a jam, sometimes fixable with a reset, sometimes a sign the motor is failing.

Leaking From The Bottom

A visibly cracked housing means full replacement, not a repair.

Frequent Jamming

Can mean a dull blade assembly or consistently the wrong items going down the drain repeatedly.

Unusual Noise Or Smell

Grinding noise or persistent odor often means something's lodged inside, or the unit is simply past its useful working life.

Caller Questions

Disposal Humming, Jammed Or Leaking?

A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. One that does nothing may have a tripped reset or a failed motor. Leaks come from the seal, the drain connection or the housing itself.

If the sink backs up when the disposal runs, the clog may be past the unit. See drain cleaning in that case.

Humming only

Jammed blades. Do not put your hand inside.

Dead unit

Check the reset button first. Otherwise the motor may have failed.

Water underneath

A leak from the housing means replacement.

Slow sink

Could be a clog beyond the disposal.

FAQs

Disposal Questions

Common questions about garbage disposal repair and replacement, including whether it's even worth fixing.

Is it worth repairing an old disposal?

Often not, given how genuinely affordable a new unit is, we'll give you an honest comparison before you decide anything.

What shouldn't go down a disposal?

Fibrous vegetables, grease, bones, and coffee grounds are all common causes of jams and premature wear over time.

Can you install a higher-horsepower unit?

Yes, if your existing electrical setup supports it, we'll confirm that carefully during the visit.

Is a new disposal noisier than my old one?

Often quieter, newer units generally run smoother and with less noise than older models nearing the end of their life.

Can a jammed garbage disposal be fixed?

Often yes. A technician frees the blades safely. Never reach into the unit, even when it is off.

When should I replace the disposal?

Replace it when the housing leaks, the motor is dead or repairs repeat.

Can you install a disposal where there is none?

Yes, if the sink and electrical setup allow. The technician confirms what is needed before starting.
