Common Problems

Signs Your Faucet Or Sink Needs Attention

Faucet and sink issues are usually straightforward once we see the actual fixture, though the visible symptom doesn't always point to the actual cause. A drip at the spout, for instance, can come from the cartridge, the supply line, or even a connection further back, which is why diagnosis comes before any parts get ordered.

Persistent Dripping

Usually a worn cartridge or washer, a low-cost repair that also stops the water waste.

Low Flow At One Fixture

Often a clogged aerator, sometimes a failing cartridge restricting flow.

Leaking Under The Sink

Supply line, P-trap, or garbage disposal connections are the usual culprits.

Loose Or Wobbly Faucet

Mounting hardware loosens over years of normal use and is a quick, inexpensive fix once identified.

What To Expect

A Faucet Or Sink Visit

We start by isolating exactly where water is coming from, the faucet itself, a supply line connection, or the sink's drain assembly, since each has a different fix. A cartridge replacement usually resolves a persistent drip in one visit. A new faucet installation includes properly sealing the connections and testing for leaks under real water pressure, not just a quick visual check. If the sink itself is failing rather than just the faucet, we'll say so and quote the full replacement rather than patching around a bigger problem.

Local Factor

Why Cartridges Wear Faster Here

Sterling draws water through Loudoun Water, sourced from the Potomac River, and homes on this supply commonly deal with moderately hard water. Mineral buildup inside a faucet cartridge is a genuine, common reason a faucet starts dripping years before it should, and it's worth mentioning if you've noticed the same faucet needing repeated repairs.

FAQs

Faucet & Sink Questions

Common questions before scheduling a faucet or sink repair or installation.

Can you install a faucet I bought myself?

Yes, we can certainly install a customer-supplied faucet, though we'll note if it looks like a lower-quality unit likely to fail early on.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing right away?

Yes, a slow drip adds up on your water bill and often gets worse, not better, over time.

Do you work on both kitchen and bathroom fixtures?

Yes, both are part of our standard service.

Can you match an existing finish or style?
