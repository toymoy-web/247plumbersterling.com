Do This First

If A Pipe Just Burst

The first two minutes matter more than anything we do after we arrive, since limiting how much water escapes before the main is shut off has a direct effect on how much gets damaged. None of these steps require any special skill, just knowing where to look before the emergency happens.

Shut Off The Main

Find your main water shutoff and turn it off immediately, this is the single most damage-limiting action you can take.

Shut Off Electricity Nearby

If water is near an outlet or panel, shut off power to that area at the breaker if you can safely reach it.

Call Us, Then Contain

Call our 24/7 line, then use towels or a wet/dry vacuum to limit how far water spreads while we're on the way.

Document For Insurance

A few photos of the damage before cleanup helps your insurance claim later.

Why This Happens

Understanding The Break

A burst pipe is rarely a random event, it's the end point of a stress that built up over time, whether that's a hard freeze expanding water inside a pipe until it splits, or years of internal corrosion thinning a galvanized line until normal water pressure was enough to finish the job.

Understanding which of these caused your specific break matters because it changes the conversation afterward: a freeze-caused break is often an isolated repair with an insulation recommendation, while a corrosion-caused break is sometimes the first sign that a longer section of pipe is close to failing too.

Caller Questions

Pipe Burst Or Cracked And Water Is Spreading?

A split copper or galvanized line, a failed supply valve or a cracked joint can release a lot of water quickly. Attic leaks and water running from a ceiling light are urgent because they travel.

The first job is stopping the flow. After that we repair or replace the failed section and check nearby pipe of the same age, since one failure often signals others.

FAQs

Burst Pipe Questions

What people ask most in the middle of a burst-pipe emergency, when there isn't time to search for answers.

I can't find my main shutoff, what do I do?

Call us immediately, we can talk you through locating it over the phone while we're on the way.

Do you handle the water damage too?

We handle the plumbing repair. For standing water and drying, see our water damage restoration service, which uses a separate dedicated line.

How fast can you get here?

Our emergency line is answered 24/7, with dispatch typically within the hour for active flooding.

Should I turn off the water myself before you arrive?

If it's actively flooding, yes, shut off the main. For a slow leak or drip, it's usually fine to wait for us.

What do I do in the first few minutes of a burst pipe?

Close the main valve, open a low faucet to relieve pressure, protect electrical areas, and call. Take photos for your records if time allows.

Can a single burst section be repaired?

Often, yes. If the surrounding pipe is old or corroded, we may recommend replacing more than the broken section. Repiping is covered on its own page.

Water is dripping from the ceiling. Is that urgent?

Yes. Water behind a ceiling can pool and bring it down. Shut the main and call right away.

Why did the pipe crack?

Age, corrosion, high pressure and freezing are the usual causes. See our frozen pipe repair page for winter failures.
