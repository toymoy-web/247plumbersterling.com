What To Do In The First Hour

How fast water is removed after a leak or flood has a real effect on how much can be saved, both structurally and financially. The steps below apply whether you're waiting for us or another crew, but the sooner they happen, the better the outcome tends to be.

Stop The Water Source

Shut off the main water valve or the specific fixture if you can safely reach it.

Avoid Standing Water Near Outlets

Don't enter a room with standing water near an electrical outlet or panel.

Move What You Can

Furniture and belongings moved off wet flooring limit secondary damage.

Call Immediately

Drying begins within hours, not days, to limit structural and mold damage.

What We Do

Extraction Through Drying

Restoration is a process, not a single visit, and we walk you through each stage.

Water Extraction

Standing water removed with commercial-grade extraction equipment.

Moisture Mapping

Moisture meters identify exactly how far water has traveled into materials, not just what's visibly wet.

Structural Drying

Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers dry affected structure, not just the surface.

Documentation For Insurance

Photos and moisture readings documented to support your insurance claim.

What To Expect

Your Restoration Timeline

Extraction starts immediately on arrival, removing standing water before it spreads further into materials. Moisture mapping follows, using meters to check how far water has traveled into walls, subfloor, and framing, not just what looks wet on the surface. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are placed based on those readings, and we return to monitor drying progress until moisture levels are back to normal, documenting each step for your insurance claim along the way.

Being Honest

What We Can And Can't Tell You

We don't make specific health claims about mold exposure, that's a conversation for your physician if you have concerns. What we can tell you honestly is the moisture readings we find, the materials affected, and the drying timeline based on the equipment in place. If mold has already visibly developed, that's a separate, specialized remediation process, which we can help you get connected to.

Local Factor

Why Speed Matters More In This Climate

Our extraction and structural drying process follows established industry drying standards for moisture mapping and equipment placement, the same general approach used across the restoration trade, applied honestly rather than rushed.

Sterling's humid subtropical climate means the conditions mold needs to develop, warmth and moisture, are present for a large part of the year, not just during a rare heat wave. Combined with a meaningful share of Sterling homes having basements or crawlspaces already prone to moisture, water damage here tends to progress toward mold risk faster than it would in a drier climate, which is exactly why our response time is built around hours, not days.
