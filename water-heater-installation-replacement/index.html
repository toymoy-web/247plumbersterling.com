When Replacement Makes Sense

Repair Vs. New Unit

Not every water heater problem calls for a full replacement, but a unit past its rated lifespan or needing a second major repair usually costs more to keep patching than to replace outright.

Tank Age Over 10-12 Years

Most tank-style units are rated for 10-12 years, and repairs on an older tank rarely pay for themselves.

Rust In The Water Or Tank

Visible rust means internal tank corrosion that a repair can't reverse.

Rising Energy Bills

An aging tank loses efficiency gradually, and a new unit often pays part of itself back in lower monthly costs.

Undersized For Your Household

A unit that was always too small for your household benefits from a properly sized replacement, not another repair.

What's Included

Every Installation Includes

What To Expect

Your Installation Day

A technician arrives, confirms the unit and location match what was quoted, and disconnects the old unit before bringing in the new one. Connections, venting, and any expansion tank are checked against current code rather than simply matched to whatever was there before, since codes have changed over the years and an older install sometimes needs a small update to pass inspection standards.

Once installed, the system is run through a full test cycle, checking temperature, pressure, and every connection for leaks, before we consider the job finished and haul away your old unit.

Tank Vs Tankless

Which Fits Your Home

Both are legitimate choices, the right one depends on your household size, budget, and how long you plan to stay in the home.

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Simpler installation
  • Limited hot water supply at once
  • 10-15 year typical lifespan

Tankless

  • Continuous hot water on demand
  • Lower monthly energy cost over time
  • Compact, wall-mounted unit
  • 20+ year typical lifespan
Local Factor

Sizing For Sterling's Mixed Housing Stock

Sterling's housing spans everything from smaller 1960s ranch homes to larger recent-construction properties, and the right water heater size genuinely differs between them. A unit sized for an older three-bedroom ranch will run short in a newer five-bathroom home with a soaking tub and a home gym shower, and we size every replacement to your household's actual fixture count and usage, not a generic default.

FAQs

Installation Questions

What Sterling homeowners ask most before scheduling a new installation, and if your question isn't covered here, ask when you call.

How long does installation take?

Most tank replacements finish the same day, often within a few hours. Tankless conversions can take longer if new venting is needed.

Do you haul away my old water heater?

Yes, removal and disposal is included in every installation.

Can I switch from tank to tankless?

Often yes, though it may require venting and gas or electrical changes we'll walk you through before quoting.

Do you install the brand I already have?

We install all major brands, so matching your existing brand or switching is both an option.

Do you offer any warranty on installation?

Workmanship on the installation itself is covered. Manufacturer warranties on the unit vary by brand and are explained before purchase.
