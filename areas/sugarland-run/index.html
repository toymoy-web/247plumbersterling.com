Local Background

Sugarland Run, A Planned Community In Loudoun County

Sugarland Run is a planned community and census-designated place in eastern Loudoun County. It uses ZIP code 20164 and a Sterling mailing address. The 2020 census counted about 12,345 residents and 3,723 housing units.

The community is named for Sugarland Run, a stream that flows toward the Potomac River along its east side. Route 7, Route 28 and the Fairfax County Parkway are the main roads nearby, which puts Reston and Herndon within easy reach of our Sterling trucks.

It is also a community with organized governance. Three homeowners associations operate there, with the Sugarland Run Homeowners Association as the main board. If your repair touches an exterior or shared element, check your own association rules first.

Plumbing Guidance

Fact, General Knowledge And What We Do Not Assume

Documented Locally

The community dates to 1971, and published descriptions say its townhouse models were built in the 1970s and 1980s. Homes of that vintage are now roughly four to five decades old.

General Plumbing Fact

Water heaters, shutoff valves, toilet parts and faucet cartridges are replaced on their own timelines. In a home that old, it is normal to find parts from several different decades in the same house.

Unknown Until Inspected

We do not know which homes have had their supply or drain lines changed. We check the house itself and explain what we see.

Because many Sugarland Run homes are townhouses, a leak can reach a neighbor through a shared wall. If that is your situation, shut off the water and tell the neighbor right away.

What ZIP code is Sugarland Run?

Check your association rules for exterior work. We plan the repair around what the job touches and can explain the scope before starting.

Turn off your main shutoff, tell the neighbor, and call us. We shut off the source first and then look at the adjoining wall.
