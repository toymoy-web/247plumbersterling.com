Chantilly Across Two Counties

Chantilly is a census-designated place in western Fairfax County, and its mailing address also reaches into Loudoun County. Two Housing Eras

How Chantilly's Plumbing Varies By Neighborhood

The county line through Chantilly also divides two very different building eras, and that changes what we expect to find before we arrive.

Established 1970s To 1990s Homes

Many Fairfax County neighborhoods in Chantilly were built between the 1970s and the 1990s and have mature trees and conventional suburban lots. Original water heaters are long gone, but supply lines, valves, and drain lines are now decades old.

The Polybutylene Era

Polybutylene supply pipe was installed in some homes nationally from roughly the late 1970s to the mid-1990s, which overlaps this building era. If you have never had your supply lines identified, a quick inspection is worthwhile.

The Newer Loudoun Side

The Loudoun County side, tied to ZIP code 20152, reflects newer master-planned development like South Riding. The plumbing there is younger, so the concerns lean toward fixtures and water heaters rather than aging pipe.

Business Parks And Airport Traffic

Chantilly also has business parks around Westfields and the Dulles Expo Center. Commercial properties here need plumbing service that is scheduled around business hours.
