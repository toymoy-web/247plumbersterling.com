Local Background

Countryside, A 1980s Community In Loudoun County

Countryside is a census-designated place in Loudoun County with 10,418 residents and 3,711 housing units in the 2020 census. The Potomac River forms its northern boundary and Route 7 forms its southern boundary. Cascades borders it to the east, and Dulles Town Center is across Route 7 to the south.

The subdivision was conceived in the mid-1970s, when about 1,000 acres of farmland were subdivided. Homes were built from 1981 through 1991, mostly by Pulte Homes in a colonial style. Most single-family homes have two stories and a basement. CountrySide Proprietary is the homeowners association.

The ZIP code is 20165, shared with Potomac Falls, and the mailing address is Sterling.

General Plumbing Fact

Basements bring floor drains, laundry hookups, sump pumps and water heaters into one space. These are common places to check after heavy rain or when a smell shows up. That is a general point about basements, not a claim about Countryside.

Unknown Until Inspected

We do not state which pipe material a Countryside house has. Builders, repairs and remodels vary, so we confirm on site.

If a basement floor drain backs up, stop using water in the house and call. Running more water often makes it worse.

What ZIP code is Countryside?

Do most Countryside homes have basements?

Most single-family homes there have two stories and a basement, according to published descriptions of the community.

Is a sump pump needed in Countryside?

We cannot say that for every home. Whether a house needs one depends on the lot and the water in the basement. We can inspect and advise.
