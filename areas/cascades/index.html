Local Background

Cascades And Lowes Island, Loudoun County

Cascades is a planned community and census-designated place in Loudoun County. It uses ZIP code 20165. The 2020 census counted 12,366 people and 4,582 housing units. The homeowners association was incorporated in 1990, and before that the land was a few very large farms.

Lowes Island at Cascades is an advertised part of the Cascades subdivision, not a separate legal subdivision. The golf course at its center is Trump National Golf Club Washington, D.C. Cascades Parkway and Palisade Parkway lead into the community, and Cascades Parkway connects to Route 7 on the south side.

Cascades is one of three main parts of the Potomac Falls community, along with Countryside and Lowes Island. Many people say Potomac Falls when they mean one of these neighborhoods, so tell us your street name and we will confirm coverage.

FAQs

Questions From Cascades Homeowners

Is Lowes Island part of Cascades?

Lowes Island at Cascades is an advertised portion of the Cascades subdivision, not a separate legal subdivision. We cover it under this page.

What ZIP code is Cascades?

Cascades uses ZIP code 20165, which is also the ZIP used for Potomac Falls. The mailing address is Sterling.

Is Cascades the same as Potomac Falls?

Cascades is one of three main parts of the Potomac Falls community. The others are Countryside and Lowes Island.

My water bill is high but nothing is leaking. What do I do?

Turn off every fixture and check whether the meter still moves. If it does, call us and describe what you see.
