South Riding, Loudoun County

South Riding is a master-planned community in southeastern Loudoun County, built out along Route 50 (Little River Turnpike) from the mid-1990s onward. Its mailing address says Chantilly and its ZIP code is 20152, which is why people often assume it sits in Fairfax County when it does not.

From our Sterling base, dispatch reaches it by way of Route 28 and Route 50 or Loudoun County Parkway, so a South Riding call is a routine run for our trucks, not a cross-county trip.

Pipe Burst In South Riding? Do This First

Shut Off The Main Valve

Locate your main water shutoff, usually where the supply line enters the home or near the water heater, and turn it fully off.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet or panel, shut off power to that area at the breaker only if you can reach it safely.

Warn Your Neighbors

Many South Riding homes are townhouses, so water can travel through a shared wall. A quick knock on the door can save a neighbor real damage.

Housing Stock

What We See In South Riding Homes

South Riding was built in phases, so a street finished in 1996 and a street finished in 2006 can need different things. These are the patterns that matter most for plumbing here.

Water Heaters Reaching Their Limit

Homes built from the mid-1990s through the 2000s are old enough that most original water heaters have been replaced once. Many of those second units are now close to the end of their rated life.

Townhome Shared Walls

A large part of South Riding is townhomes. A leak in one unit can reach a neighbor's ceiling or wall quickly, so speed and a clear shutoff plan matter more here.

Fixtures Aging Out

Toilet fill valves, faucet cartridges, and outdoor hose bibs on 15 to 30 year old homes are the small parts that tend to fail first. They are quick, low-cost repairs when caught early.

Association Guidelines

South Riding is governed by the South Riding Proprietary, and exterior work may need to follow association guidelines. We plan around that so a repair does not turn into a compliance question.

FAQs

Questions From South Riding Homeowners

The most common things South Riding homeowners ask before booking, including how South Riding differs from Chantilly.

Do you cover every ZIP code in South Riding?

Yes, South Riding is covered by ZIP code 20152, which includes the neighborhoods along Tall Cedars Parkway and Poland Road and the townhome sections near Riding Center Drive. That ZIP is shared with the Loudoun County side of Chantilly, and we cover both.

Is emergency service available in South Riding?

Yes, our emergency line runs 24/7 and is dispatched to South Riding from Sterling, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Do you work with the South Riding homeowners association?

Yes, South Riding is governed by the South Riding Proprietary, and we plan around association guidelines when a repair touches exterior or shared elements.

Is South Riding the same as Chantilly?

They share the Chantilly mailing address and ZIP code 20152, but South Riding sits in Loudoun County while Chantilly proper is in Fairfax County. We cover both, and each has its own page.

Do you service townhomes with shared walls?

Yes, and we treat leaks in a townhome with extra urgency because water can reach a neighbor quickly. We shut off the source first, then check the shared wall and ceiling for moisture.
