Emergency Vs. Scheduled

Which Kind Of Call Is This?

Some of what's listed above is a same-day scheduled visit, and some is a genuine emergency, and the difference matters for how fast we move. Burst pipes, sewage backups, and total loss of hot water go on the 24/7 emergency line immediately. Fixture repairs, installations, and routine drain cleaning get a scheduled window that usually still lands the same day if you call before 2pm.
