Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This policy explains how Sterling 24/7 Plumbing collects, uses, and protects information.

Information We Collect

What We Collect

When you call or email us, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, and service address, in order to schedule and complete plumbing work. We do not use website forms. All contact happens by phone or email.

How We Use It

Use Of Information

Information you provide is used only to schedule, perform, and follow up on plumbing service. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
