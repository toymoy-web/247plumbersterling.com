Common Culprits

What's Actually Clogging Sterling Drains

The right fix depends on what's actually causing the clog, not just where it is. Here's what we find most often, and why treating the symptom instead of the cause tends to bring the same clog back within a few months.

Grease Buildup In Kitchen Lines

Cooking grease cools and hardens inside the pipe wall, narrowing the line a little more with every use until flow slows to a crawl.

Tree Roots In Older Sewer Laterals

Roots finding their way into aging joints is a well-documented recurring cause of slow drains across this part of Loudoun County.

Hair & Soap In Bathroom Drains

The most common tub and shower clog, usually clearable with a standard snake.

Mineral Scale In Older Pipe

Scale buildup inside older pipe narrows the usable diameter over years, not overnight, and gets worse the longer it's left alone.

Two Levels Of Clean

Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting

These aren't interchangeable, they solve different problems, and picking the wrong one either wastes money or leaves the real cause untouched. Here's how we decide which your line actually needs.

Cable / Snake Clearing

  • Cuts a path through the immediate clog
  • Faster and lower cost
  • Good for a single, isolated blockage
  • Doesn't clean the full pipe wall

Hydro Jetting

  • Scours the entire interior pipe wall
  • Clears grease buildup and root intrusion
  • Longer-lasting result on recurring clogs
  • Camera-verified before and after

Which Fits Your Home

A camera-verified diagnosis, not a guess, tells us which of these two methods actually solves your specific line's problem.

Local Factor

Pipe Material Changes The Approach

Sterling's kitchen and bathroom drain lines vary by when a given section of the city was built. Older-phase homes often run cast iron drain lines, which hold up differently under high-pressure jetting than the PVC found in newer townhomes and additions.

We check pipe material and condition on camera before jetting anything, since a genuinely deteriorated cast-iron line needs a gentler approach or a repair conversation instead of full-pressure jetting.

Caller Questions

Drain Backing Up And Nothing Is Working?

When a sink, tub and toilet back up together, the clog is usually in the main line rather than one fixture. Gurgling toilets and water seeping near baseboards or a basement floor drain are common signs.

Snaking clears many stoppages. Hydro jetting washes out grease, scale and roots from the pipe walls. If a snake stalls partway or the clog keeps returning, a camera inspection shows what is really in the line.

FAQs

Drain Cleaning Questions

Whether jetting is overkill for your situation is the question we hear most, along with these, and we're happy to explain the reasoning behind whichever method we recommend.

Do I need jetting or just a snake?

A camera check tells us which is actually needed, we won't sell jetting for a simple hair clog.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipe?

We check pipe condition on camera first, since genuinely fragile old pipe needs a gentler approach.

How often should main lines be jetted?

Homes with a known root or grease issue often benefit from a check every 1-2 years, otherwise it's as-needed.

Will jetting damage an old cast-iron line?

Not if the pipe is structurally sound, which is exactly what the camera inspection checks before we recommend it. If a section looks thin or fragile, we'll say so and suggest a gentler approach instead.

Snaking or hydro jetting. Which one do I need?

Snaking opens a clog. Jetting scours the pipe wall and suits grease, scale and root growth. The technician recommends one after seeing how the line behaves.

The clog keeps coming back. Why?

A repeat clog often means roots, a belly in the pipe or buildup that a snake only punches through. A camera inspection shows the cause. See sewer line repair if the pipe is damaged.

Can you come today for a backed up main line?

Call the 24/7 line and the dispatcher will confirm timing. Mention if sewage is coming up so it is handled as urgent.

Do you clean outdoor and yard drains?

Tell the dispatcher about roots or debris in exterior drains. We will confirm whether it fits our drain service before booking.
