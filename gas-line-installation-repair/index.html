Safety First

If You Smell Gas

A suspected gas leak is one of the few plumbing situations where the immediate safety steps matter more than anything else, more than diagnosing the cause, more than saving time. Getting these right costs nothing and takes seconds, which is exactly why they come first here.

Do Not Operate Switches

Don't flip light switches, plug in anything, or use your phone inside the affected area.

Leave The Area Immediately

Get everyone out of the house, don't wait to investigate the source yourself.

Call From Outside

Call us and your gas utility's emergency line from a safe distance, not from inside the home.

Don't Re-Enter Until Cleared

Wait for confirmation the area is safe before going back inside.

What To Expect

A Gas Line Service Visit

For a suspected leak, we locate the source using proper detection equipment, never by smell alone, and repair it to code before pressure-testing the line. For a new appliance connection, we carefully run the line, make the connection, and test under pressure before the appliance is ever turned on. Gas work is permitted and formally inspected in most jurisdictions, and we always handle that coordination as part of the job so it isn't ever left for you to manage separately.

Local Factor

Gas Service Across Sterling's Housing Stock

All gas line work follows National Fuel Gas Code guidelines and local permitting requirements, with every line pressure-tested before use.

Sterling's mixed-era construction means gas line age and routing varies significantly by when a given section was built. Older homes sometimes have gas lines that predate a kitchen or laundry remodel and need extending or rerouting to reach a relocated appliance, which we plan for during the initial assessment rather than discovering mid-job.

FAQs

Gas Line Questions

Common questions about gas line service and safety, since this is one area where getting it right really matters.

Is gas line work permitted separately?

Yes, gas line work typically requires its own permit and inspection, which we handle as part of the job.

Can you add a gas line for a new range or dryer?

Yes, running a brand new appliance connection is a common request we handle quite regularly.

What if I'm not sure it's actually a gas smell?

Treat it as one anyway regardless, leave the area immediately and call, it's far better to have a false alarm checked than to ignore a real leak.

Do you offer any warranty on gas line work?

Yes, workmanship on gas line installation and repair is covered; we'll explain the specific terms.
