Warning Signs

Signs Of A Slab Leak

A slab leak hides beneath your foundation, so the signs are indirect but real, showing up as symptoms elsewhere rather than an obvious wet spot where the actual problem is. Recognizing the pattern early is what keeps this a plumbing repair instead of a foundation problem.

Warm Or Wet Spot On The Floor

A hot water line leak often creates a noticeably warm patch on an otherwise cool floor.

Cracks In Flooring Or Walls

Sustained water under a slab can shift the foundation enough to crack tile or drywall above it.

Unexplained Water Bill Spike

A slab leak runs continuously, which shows up fast on a water bill.

Low Water Pressure

Water escaping under the slab means less pressure reaching your fixtures.

What To Expect

Safety First

What To Do Before We Arrive

Avoid The Warm Or Wet Spot

If the leak is on a hot water line, the affected area can be hot enough to cause discomfort or minor burns on bare feet.

Keep Electronics Away From Damp Flooring

Don't place cords, power strips, or plugged-in devices on a floor area showing signs of moisture.

Note When You First Noticed It

A rough timeline helps us and your insurance understand how long the leak has likely been active.

Repair Options

Reroute Vs. Direct Repair

Once located, there are genuinely two honest paths forward, and the right one depends on the pipe's condition and location.

Pipe Rerouting

  • Avoids breaking the slab entirely
  • New line run through walls or ceiling
  • Best for isolated single-line leaks

Direct Slab Repair

  • Targeted opening at the exact leak point
  • Shorter-term cost, same pipe material
  • Better when multiple lines are affected
Local Factor

Why Sterling Sees Slab Movement

Any slab leak repair, whether a reroute or a direct repair, is completed to Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standards for pipe material and connections.

Sterling sits where Loudoun County's Piedmont clay soils transition toward coastal sand formations, and that shift causes real, ongoing ground movement rather than a one-time settling event. Pipes embedded in a slab poured decades ago are more exposed to this kind of stress than newer construction using more flexible pipe materials, which is part of why slab leaks recur in some sections of Sterling more than others.

FAQs

Slab Leak Questions

Common questions before scheduling a slab leak repair, since it's a bigger decision than a typical repair call.

Will you have to break up my floor?

Not necessarily, rerouting the pipe above the slab is often possible and avoids breaking concrete.

How urgent is a slab leak really?

Genuinely urgent, ongoing water under a foundation can cause structural issues the longer it's left unaddressed.

Does homeowner's insurance cover this?

Often partially, coverage varies by policy; we can provide documentation to support a claim.

Can I stay in my home during slab leak repair?

In most cases yes, work is typically contained to one area and doesn't require you to leave.
